A few things
worth knowing.

The practical details, in plain language.

What is SimProof, in simple terms?

It is a checking tool for engineering simulations: the computer models engineers use to predict heat, forces or vibration. It looks for mistakes in the setup and results, and shows where the numbers in its report came from.

Does it replace an engineer’s review?

No. It supports your review with repeatable checks and traceable findings. Engineering judgement is still needed to decide whether the model represents the real design and whether the design is safe.

Which solvers can I use today?

Ansys Mechanical and MAPDL are the main workflow, including input decks, solver output and .rth or .rst results. Abaqus, Nastran and CalculiX readers are early support and have not yet been validated on real solver output. See the current scope.

What happens to my study files?

For the pilot, we sign an NDA first, or use yours. Checks run offline on our machine and the files are deleted within 7 days. Please use the form only to describe your study; we will agree a file-transfer method with you.

What do I get from the free pilot?

A check of one study, a report with the findings and their evidence, and a 20-minute review call. In return, we ask for feedback on whether the report helps your workflow.
