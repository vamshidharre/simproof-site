05 Examples

What SimProof finds.

Four synthetic test models. Every number below is what SimProof printed.

Fails

Pulsed heat loads in a steady-state solve

Ansys Mechanical, thermal

What was checked

A heat study whose loads switch on and off over time, solved as steady state at t = 0.012 s.

SimProof found

0.000
mW/mm² applied at the solve
33.33
mW/mm² average over time
80.00
mW/mm² peak

The solve looks at the load only at the instant it is switched off, so this heat input counts as zero. If an average load was intended, apply the average.

Fails

A part that nothing holds

Ansys Mechanical, static structural

What was checked

A two-part model with one support. Only one of the parts is connected to it.

SimProof found

2
parts in the mesh
1
part connected to a support
1
part with no path to a support

One part floats free, so its results are not meaningful. SimProof names a node in that part (node 11) so the engineer can find it.

Needs review

A vibration study where the part can drift

Ansys Mechanical, modal

What was checked

A natural-frequency study that solved seven modes without any error.

SimProof found

6
modes below 0.001 Hz
12 Hz
first real vibration mode
1
support definition

Six near-zero frequencies usually mean the part can move as a whole. Confirm it is meant to be free, or add the supports that were intended.
